What does SPACE AI LABS build?

What does supporting 39 languages actually cost?

ChartMind ships in 39 languages, including Arabic and Hebrew with full right-to-left layout. Real localisation changes the typography, not just the strings: Arabic is cursive, so letter-spacing breaks the joins and emphasis has to move from italic to weight.

Most apps translate a string file and call it localisation. The part nobody checks is what happens to layout, numerals, date order and text direction — and that is the part that decides whether the app feels native or merely available.
